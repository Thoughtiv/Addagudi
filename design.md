# Addagudi Exports Pvt. Ltd. --- Landing Page Design Specification

## 1. Project Overview

Create a polished, responsive corporate landing page for **Addagudi
Exports Pvt. Ltd. (AEPL)**, an engineering, manufacturing, and export
company based in Hyderabad, India.

The page should communicate precision engineering, dependable
manufacturing, quality assurance, and long-term customer partnerships.
The visual direction is inspired by modern industrial editorial
websites: oversized condensed headlines, high-impact machinery
photography, restrained technical annotations, structured capability
cards, numbered process stages, and a confident dark footer.

**Important:** Use the supplied modern reference image as the design
direction. Do not use the old AEPL website screenshots as design
references. Those screenshots are a source of business information only.

## 2. Brand and Visual Direction

### Overall feel

-   Modern, premium, technical, industrial, and precise.
-   Strong editorial hierarchy with bold, condensed headlines.
-   Generous whitespace and clean alignment.
-   Large, high-quality manufacturing photography.
-   Technical details used as accents rather than decoration everywhere.
-   Clear conversion paths for enquiries and product/capability
    exploration.

### Color palette

Use blue as a deliberate accent, not as a full-page background. Keep
body text dark and readable on light sections. Dark sections should use
white or cool-gray text.

### Typography

-   Use a bold, tightly tracked condensed display face for headlines.
    Suitable options include **Barlow Condensed**, **Roboto Condensed**,
    or **Oswald**.
-   Use a clean sans-serif for paragraphs, labels, and navigation, such
    as **Inter** or **Manrope**.
-   Headlines should be uppercase, heavy, compact, and broken into
    intentional lines.
-   Use small uppercase technical labels with moderate letter spacing.
-   Avoid decorative serif fonts and overly rounded display type.
-   If using a web font, provide a sensible system fallback.

Suggested hierarchy: - Hero headline: very large, bold, condensed;
responsive `clamp()` sizing. - Section headlines: large condensed
display style. - Card titles: compact uppercase display style. - Body
copy: comfortable line-height around 1.45--1.65. - Micro-labels: small
uppercase text with increased letter spacing.

## 3. Page Structure

Build the page as a single long-form homepage with the following
sections in order.

### A. Header / Navigation

A slim white header with: - AEPL logo/wordmark on the left. - Navigation
links: Home, About, Products, Machinery, Quality, Contact. - A compact
right-aligned CTA: **Enquire Now**. - Optional small supporting line:
"Manufacturing Custom Components Since 1997".

Behavior: - Keep the header clean and low-profile. - On mobile, collapse
navigation into an accessible menu. - The CTA should navigate to the
enquiry/contact section. - Use the real AEPL logo asset if provided. If
no clean logo asset is available, use a temporary text-based AEPL
wordmark without inventing a new corporate logo.

### B. Hero --- Precision-Engineered Components

Use a two-part hero composition: - Left side: small technical eyebrow,
large multi-line headline, short company introduction, primary CTA and
secondary link. - Right side: large hero photograph of CNC machining, a
precision-turned component, or industrial manufacturing equipment. - Add
a few fine blue technical callouts over the image, such as "CNC
Precision Machining", "Custom Components", and "Quality Inspection".
Keep these subtle and legible.

Suggested headline: **ENGINEERED COMPONENTS FOR A STRONGER TOMORROW.**

Highlight "TOMORROW." in steel blue.

Suggested supporting copy: "Addagudi Exports Pvt. Ltd. (AEPL) is an
engineering and manufacturing company producing custom-made engineered
components for global industries."

Primary CTA: **Enquire Now**\
Secondary CTA: **View Products**

A compact proof strip may show: - Established 1997 - Engineering &
Manufacturing - Quality-Focused Production

Do not claim unverified precision tolerances, production capacity,
customer counts, or certifications as current facts.

### C. Industry / Capability Strip

Place a full-width dark strip directly after the hero with four concise
capability labels, optionally paired with simple line icons: - Custom
Engineered Components - CNC Machining - OEM / Assembly Support - Quality
Inspection

Use thin vertical dividers on desktop and a responsive wrap/stack layout
on smaller screens.

### D. Capabilities --- From Raw Material to Finished Component

Capabilities" - Large headline: **FROM RAW MATERIAL TO FINISHED
COMPONENT.** - A short supporting paragraph aligned opposite the

custom components and complex geometries." - Link label: "Explore

Card 02 --- **Component Manufacturing** - Copy: "Custom-made engineered

Card 03 --- **Quality & Inspection** - Copy: "Inspection and quality

borders, and minimal shadows. Do not imply that all inspection is

### E. Products --- Precision Components Across Industries

Use a dark charcoal/near-black section to provide visual contrast.

Layout: - Left: section eyebrow, oversized headline, short paragraph,

Suggested headline: **PRECISION COMPONENTS ACROSS INDUSTRIES.**

Suggested copy: "AEPL manufactures turned and machined components

Product categories: - Turned Components - Machined Parts - Custom

### G. Process --- A Streamlined Route to Quality

inspection equipment, and third-party inspection when required."

Verify current certification status and validity. - Use a current

prominent certification claim. - Never imply that ISO 9001:2008 is a
current certification based solely on the old screenshot.

## 4. Background Rhythm and Section Differentiation

The homepage should have a deliberate rhythm of light and dark
surfaces: 1. White header and hero. 2. Near-black capability strip. 3.
White capabilities section. 4. Near-black product showcase. 5. White
machinery section. 6. Pale steel-blue process section with subtle
industrial imagery or texture. 7. White/cool-white quality and
certification section. 8. Near-black footer with restrained blue
technical detailing.

The final process and footer sections must not look like identical
blocks. The process section should feel light, technical, and open; the
footer should feel dark, grounded, and compact.

## 5. Imagery Guidance

Prioritize authentic AEPL images: - CNC lathe and milling machines. -
Metal turning and machining in progress. - Precision-turned metallic
components. - Finished machined parts and assemblies. - Inspection
equipment and measurement activity. - Factory or manufacturing-floor
images. - Authentic quality certificates, where current and verified.

Image treatment: - Use large, clean crops with consistent aspect
ratios. - Keep machinery photography realistic and professional. -
Prefer neutral metallic tones with blue highlights. - Use image overlays
only where text needs them. - Add useful alt text describing the
image. - Optimize images and use responsive sizes.

If temporary stock images are used during development, replace them with
real company photography before launch. Do not present generated imagery
as actual AEPL facility or equipment photography.

## 6. Interaction and Motion

Keep motion subtle and purposeful: - Smooth anchor navigation. - Small
hover transitions on buttons and links. - Gentle reveal-on-scroll for
section content, with reduced-motion support. - Slight image scale on
hover for capability cards, without affecting layout. - Clear focus
states for keyboard users.

Avoid excessive parallax, autoplay video with sound, distracting
animation, and motion that delays access to content.

## 7. Responsive Behavior

### Desktop

-   Wide editorial layout with two-column hero.
-   Large machinery image on the right.
-   Three capability cards in a row.
-   Product section with copy, image, and category list.
-   Machinery cards in a horizontal grid.
-   Process stages in a single horizontal line.

### Tablet

-   Preserve headline hierarchy while reducing font sizes.
-   Use two-column capability and machinery grids where appropriate.
-   Allow hero content and image to rebalance vertically.

### Mobile

-   Stack hero copy and image.
-   Keep the main CTA visible without crowding.
-   Collapse navigation into an accessible menu.
-   Stack capability cards and machinery cards.
-   Convert the process timeline to a vertical sequence.
-   Prevent horizontal overflow from technical labels or tables.
-   Keep body copy comfortably readable and buttons easy to tap.

## 8. Accessibility and Quality Requirements

-   Use semantic landmarks and a logical heading hierarchy.
-   Ensure sufficient contrast for text, controls, and blue accents.
-   Provide keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
-   Give meaningful images appropriate alt text; mark purely decorative
    imagery accordingly.
-   Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
-   Make all CTA labels clear and specific.
-   Ensure mobile layouts do not require horizontal scrolling.
-   Optimize image formats, loading, and layout stability.
-   Avoid unverified business claims and outdated certification
    statements.

## 9. Content Accuracy Rules

Use the following confirmed company facts: - Addagudi Exports Pvt.
Ltd. (AEPL). - Established in 1997 in Hyderabad, India. - Engineering,
manufacturing, and export of custom-made engineered components. -
CNC-turned and machined metallic components. - Work ranging from
individual components to finished assembly/OEM requirements. - Quality
practices described in the historical site include manufacturing-stage
checks, first-article reports, calibrated inspection equipment,
documented procedures, and third-party inspection when required. - A
historical ISO 9001:2008 certificate is shown in the supplied content. -
Machinery names and models as listed in the source content.

Do not invent: - Customer logos or testimonials. - Exact production
capacity, facility area, number of CNC cells, or employee count. -
Precision tolerances or guaranteed lead times. - Current certification
status. - Named global customers or specific export destinations. -
Unverified industry specializations.

## 10. Suggested Implementation Notes

-   Build reusable components for Header, Button, SectionEyebrow,
    CapabilityCard, MachineryCard, ProcessStep, and Footer.
-   Store repeated content such as machinery and capability cards in
    structured arrays.
-   Use CSS variables/design tokens for colors, spacing, borders, and
    typography.
-   Use responsive CSS Grid and Flexbox instead of fixed-width
    positioning.
-   Keep section spacing consistent while allowing the hero and product
    showcase to have stronger visual emphasis.
-   Make all navigation and CTA links functional.
-   Connect the enquiry form to a real backend or email service before
    production.
-   Add metadata, a descriptive page title, social sharing metadata, and
    search-friendly page copy.
-   Test at mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints.
